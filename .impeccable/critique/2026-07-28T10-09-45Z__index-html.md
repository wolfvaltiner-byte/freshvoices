## Overall Impression

The site's core deliverable — sound — is handled with real craft: a custom scrubbable audio player with correct ARIA semantics, a distinctive waveform motif, and copy that directly defuses the visitor's actual hesitation ("kein Download, kein Formular. Einfach anhören."). What holds it back from "Good→Excellent" is that the site doesn't fully believe its own promises yet: it sells Dokumentation and E-Learning with real prices but shows zero proof of either; it claims frictionless bilingual browsing but the About page's biography — the most personal, highest-trust content on the site — silently reverts to German for exactly the returning English-preferring visitor the persistence feature was built for. The single biggest opportunity is closing that promise-to-proof gap (samples for every priced service) and finishing the bilingual system's last mile (About page, nav, footer) rather than adding anything new.

## What's Working

1. **The "no gate" copy on samples.html is a stated design principle turned into literal, load-bearing UI copy** — most portfolio sites gesture at "listen to my work"; this one names the visitor's actual fear (being asked for an email first) in the subhead exactly where that fear would occur.
2. **The waveform motif is a genuine, low-cost signature device** that ties every page back to "this is about sound," used as section dividers and echoed in the player's own progress bar — something an unrelated business literally could not lift.
3. **The custom audio player is built, not just styled** — single-active-player enforcement, keyboard-scrubbable progress with a Shift-modifier for larger jumps, and correct `role="slider"` ARIA semantics go well past what a portfolio site typically bothers to implement for its own core deliverable.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] Impressum/Datenschutzerklärung links are dead on every page — worst possible placement, right below the contact form
**Why it matters**: this isn't a nice-to-have for an Austrian business — Impressum is a legal disclosure requirement — and a broken privacy-policy link sitting directly under a form collecting name/email/company/project details hits a privacy-conscious visitor at the exact moment of highest intent.
**Fix**: build minimal Impressum/Datenschutz pages before any public launch — this is a launch blocker.
**Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

### [P1] About page's EN persistence is broken for the main body content — contradicts the site's own "language persists across pages" claim
**Why it matters**: `main.js` swaps every `[data-de]/[data-en]` element and reads the stored `fv-lang` preference on load, but about.html's biography lives in separate `[data-lang-block]` wrappers toggled only by a duplicate inline script that fires on click, never on load — and due to listener-registration order, that script reads stale button text, so it takes two clicks to reach a fully consistent language state. An EN-preferring returning visitor lands on the site's most personal page and finds the whole life story still in German.
**Fix**: fold the `data-lang-block` toggle into `main.js`'s single `applyLang()` function; delete the duplicate inline script.
**Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

### [P1] Samples page has zero audio proof for two of the six services actually sold
**Why it matters**: Dokumentation and E-Learning & Training are priced on the homepage and services.html, but the samples filter has no category or group for either — the product's stated success condition ("a visitor hears samples, feels confident in quality") fails specifically for the two highest-ticket B2B categories.
**Fix**: add real samples for these categories, or stop promising/pricing them until proof exists — selling in two places and proving in zero is the worst combination.
**Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

### [P2] Trust-strip artifacts on clients.html look unfinished on the one page whose job is to look finished
**Why it matters**: an unlinked "Logo Banner" image (every other logo in the row is a clickable link) and a dashed-border placeholder box reading the literal text "VOICE" instead of an actual association logo both read as debug/staging leftovers on the credibility page.
**Fix**: resolve the Bormes-les-Mimosas duplicate-logo decision and swap in the real VOICE logo.
**Suggested command**: `/impeccable polish`

### [P2] Contact form front-loads 6 fields with submit-only validation — in tension with the site's own "lower the barrier" principle
**Why it matters**: the site otherwise goes out of its way to remove friction (no-gate listening, one-click language toggle); the one place asking the most of a visitor gives the least real-time guidance, so mistakes surface only after clicking submit.
**Fix**: add `blur`/`input`-level validation feedback per field.
**Suggested command**: `/impeccable clarify`
